## Root cause found (new)
Disk deficit is **not** the Chrome `.pma` spool. Measured 6 files / 24.0 MiB vs the recorded 101 / 404 MiB, and 0.00 MiB/day on every large path over a 120s window. Real driver: the 6-hourly backup rewriting `/root/backups/chronicle.db` (3.22 GiB) + `state.db` (758 MiB). `rr_backup_system.sh:39` consumes `chronicle.db` as the scp source for the DreamHost offsite RPO — **not deletable**. `prune-backups.sh` has no rule for loose `*.db` copies, so that dir can never be retention-bounded.

**Generalizable lesson — a linear extrapolation from a short window is not a rate.** The 6.92 GiB/day figure came from a 200s sample and was written into a record and carried to a human as fact; the next scan falsified it. Growth rates written into issues/journals must be burst-aware (sample a full recurring window, or record per-run deltas), or explicitly labeled as a short-window sample.
